# Themis Knowledge

## Responsibility

Govern durable, reusable lessons derived from project evidence. Knowledge owns candidate analysis and human disposition, while authoritative project facts remain in approved `workspace/context/`.

## Inputs

- outcomes, runs, evidence, implementation observations, and human submissions;
- existing Context and relevant current code/configuration/Schema;
- candidates and governance records beneath `workspace/knowledge/`.

## Outputs and flow

- persist candidates, source refs, fact validation, duplicate/conflict analysis, recommendation, review, and human decision under `workspace/knowledge/`;
- only after explicit approval, apply the declared Context/Catalog change;
- reread the result and preserve apply evidence;
- without actual apply and reread, keep disposition `pending` and do not claim promotion.

## Boundaries

- Do not store project knowledge in Core or create a second formal knowledge database.
- Do not promote secrets, transient logs, unverified observations, or facts with unresolved conflicts.
- Do not bypass duplicate, conflict, scope, stability, provenance, or human approval checks.
- Do not claim atomicity, idempotence, rollback, recovery, digest, or promotion without observed runtime/tool evidence.
